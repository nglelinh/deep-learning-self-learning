# Course introduction

Deep learning is a central tool of modern artificial intelligence: vision, translation, generative media, control, and many other applications. This course is designed for **systematic self-study**, moving from mathematical and neural-network foundations to modern architectures (CNNs, RNNs/LSTMs, attention, Transformers), then to generative models, transfer learning, and broader applications.

## Audience and approach

Learners should know basic calculus, linear algebra, probability, and Python. Each lesson is written as a **full lecture note**: intuition, mathematics, numerical examples, code sketches, foundational papers, and common pitfalls.

Content is organized by chapter; long topics are split into smaller lessons for readability. The sidebar supports collapsing and expanding chapters and nested sections.

## Two language editions

The main curriculum is available in **English and Vietnamese**. Editions are aligned by `chapter` and `order`, so you can switch language on any lesson without losing your place. The Vietnamese edition uses academic textbook style; technical terms are introduced in Vietnamese with English glosses when helpful.

## Suggested path

1. **Chapter 00** — math refreshers (if needed).  
2. **Chapters 01–03** — what deep learning is, neural nets, training.  
3. **Chapters 04–08** — CNNs, sequences, LSTM, attention, Transformers.  
4. **Chapters 09–10** — regularization and optimization.  
5. **Chapters 11–15** — generative models and transfer learning.  
6. **Chapters 16–25** — applications and future directions.

Happy learning.
