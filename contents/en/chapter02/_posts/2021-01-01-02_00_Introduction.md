Neural Networks are the foundation of modern deep learning. This chapter introduces the basic building blocks of neural networks, including neurons, layers, activation functions, and forward propagation. We'll explore how these simple units combine to create powerful learning systems capable of solving complex problems.

![Handwritten digits on pixel grids](/deep-learning-self-learning/img/chapter_img/chapter02/mnist_digits_grid.jpg)
*Figure: Handwritten digit recognition — each image is a pixel grid. (Illustration from an introductory neural-network video)*

Understanding neural networks is essential for anyone working in deep learning, as they form the basis for more advanced architectures and techniques covered in later chapters.

### Quick intuition: from pixels to predictions

For digit images (e.g. a $$28 \times 28$$ grid), we often **flatten** the image into a vector of $$784$$ numbers and pass it through stacked neuron layers. Each layer transforms the representation; the final layer can produce $$10$$ outputs for digits $$0$$–$$9$$.

![From a 28×28 image to a multilayer network](/deep-learning-self-learning/img/chapter_img/chapter02/mnist_to_mlp_layers.jpg)
*Figure: Image $$28\times28$$ → $$784$$ inputs → hidden layers → outputs. (Illustration from an introductory neural-network video)*
