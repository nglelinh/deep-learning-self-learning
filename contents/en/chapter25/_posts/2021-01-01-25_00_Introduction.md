This chapter explores cutting-edge research and future directions in deep learning: multimodal models (CLIP, Flamingo), neural architecture search, meta-learning (learning to learn), continual learning, federated learning, neuromorphic computing, quantum machine learning, and emerging challenges like AI safety, fairness, and sustainability. We look at where deep learning is heading and open research problems.



**Optional (2022–2026):** After the future-directions survey, see **25-99 Modern Applications and Updates** for test-time compute, agents, and open VLMs that already shipped.

**Optional (interview practice):** After the advanced-topics survey, see **25-98 Interviews Practice** for original Bayesian / uncertainty prompts; open *Deep Learning Interviews* ([arXiv:2201.00650](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.00650)) for the solved bank.

**Dedicated LLM hub:** Pretraining, prompting, alignment, serving, and distillation of large language models are in **Chapter 26**, not in this survey. The qualitative Chinchilla-style compute-optimal sketch (Kaplan / Hoffmann, no invented curves) is **26-07**.
