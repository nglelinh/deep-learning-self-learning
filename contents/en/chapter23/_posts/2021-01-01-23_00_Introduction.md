Efficient deep learning makes models faster, smaller, and more deployable. This chapter covers model compression (pruning, quantization, knowledge distillation), efficient architectures (MobileNet, EfficientNet), hardware acceleration (GPUs, TPUs), mixed-precision training, and deployment strategies for edge devices and production systems.



**Optional (2022–2026):** After pruning/quantization/distillation, see **23-99 Modern Applications and Updates** for GPTQ, AWQ, GGUF, and speculative decoding.

**LLM distillation hub:** Classical KD in **23-01** is the fixed-class recipe. For modern LLM students (synthetic data, white-box features/logits, serving-vs-distill decisions) see **Chapter 26**. Speculative decoding as a *serving* loop (not a student) is **26-04**; FLOP / KV accounting is **26-07**.
