Generative models learn to create new data samples similar to training data. This chapter introduces the fundamentals of generative modeling, probability distributions, maximum likelihood estimation, and evaluation metrics. These concepts form the foundation for autoencoders, VAEs, and GANs.



**Optional (2022–2026):** After the generative-model fundamentals, see **11-99 Modern Applications and Updates** for the shift to diffusion and flow matching (VAE/GAN details stay in later chapters).
