Humans do not need a million labeled flash cards to learn that a rotated coffee cup is still the same cup, or that the missing word in *"The cat sat on the ___"* is probably *mat*. Self-Supervised Learning (SSL) unlocks the vast ocean of unlabeled text, images, audio, and video by inventing **pretext tasks** whose labels come for free from the data itself—turning raw structure into supervision signals powerful enough to rival and surpass supervised pretraining.

## Key ideas: Foundations of Self-Supervised Learning

- **Pillar 1 — Pretext Tasks & Invariance Design**: Crafts supervision directly from raw inputs (masked patches, next tokens, augmented views) so solving the pretext task forces the encoder to capture high-level semantics.
- **Pillar 2 — Contrastive Learning (InfoNCE)**: Pulls representations of two augmented views of the same image together while pushing all other images in the batch apart on the unit hypersphere.
- **Pillar 3 — Generative / Masked Prediction (MLM & MAE)**: Hides a fraction of input tokens or patches and trains the network to reconstruct them from context, scaling effortlessly without large negative-sample banks.
- **Pillar 4 — Collapse Prevention Mechanisms**: Explains why naive positive-pair matching collapses to a constant vector $f(x) \equiv c$ and how negatives, stop-gradients, momentum encoders, and variance-covariance regularizers prevent it.

### Worked example: Computing InfoNCE Loss on One Positive and Two Negatives

Suppose an anchor embedding $z_i$, its positive augmented view $z_j$, and two negative images $z_{k_1}, z_{k_2}$ are L2-normalized with cosine similarities:
$$s_{ij} = 0.80, \qquad s_{i, k_1} = 0.20, \qquad s_{i, k_2} = -0.10, \qquad \text{temperature } \tau = 0.5.$$

1. **Scale similarities by $1/\tau = 2.0$**:
   $$s_{ij}/\tau = 1.60, \qquad s_{i,k_1}/\tau = 0.40, \qquad s_{i,k_2}/\tau = -0.20.$$
2. **Exponentiate each scaled logit**:
   $$e^{1.60} \approx 4.953, \qquad e^{0.40} \approx 1.492, \qquad e^{-0.20} \approx 0.819.$$
3. **Compute InfoNCE probability and loss**:
   $$p_{ij} = \frac{4.953}{4.953 + 1.492 + 0.819} = \frac{4.953}{7.264} \approx 0.6819.$$
   $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}} = -\ln(0.6819) \approx 0.3829\text{ nats}.$$
4. **Takeaway**: InfoNCE is literally cross-entropy classification over the batch, and lowering temperature $\tau$ sharpens the softmax so the model focuses its gradients on the *hardest* negatives (those with highest $s_{ik}$).

Self-supervised learning trains models using automatically generated labels from the data itself, without manual annotation. This paradigm has revolutionized pre-training in NLP (masked language modeling) and computer vision (contrastive learning). This chapter covers contrastive learning, SimCLR, CLIP, MAE, and modern self-supervised approaches.
