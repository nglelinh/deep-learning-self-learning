Regularization prevents overfitting by constraining model complexity. This chapter covers essential techniques: L1/L2 regularization, dropout, batch normalization, data augmentation, and early stopping. These methods are crucial for building models that generalize well to unseen data.



**Optional (2022–2026):** After dropout and BatchNorm, see **09-99 Modern Applications and Updates** for SAM, DropPath, and how foundation-model recipes regularize today.
